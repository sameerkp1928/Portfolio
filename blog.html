Life at IIT Roorkee: More Than Just Academics

October 2026 · 3 min read

Life at IIT Roorkee is more than lectures, assignments, and examinations. It is about discovering new interests, meeting people from different backgrounds, and exploring opportunities beyond the classroom.

The campus itself has a unique charm. The historic James Thomason Building reflects the institute's rich legacy, while the Mahatma Gandhi Central Library provides a space for learning and exploration. Walking through the green pathways between classes offers a refreshing break from academic life.

For students, places like the Multi Activity Centre (MAC) are important social hubs. Whether grabbing a snack, catching up with friends, or discussing an interesting idea, these everyday moments often become memorable parts of college life. Hostel life adds another dimension, bringing together students with different perspectives, ambitions, and experiences.

Beyond academics, IIT Roorkee offers opportunities to explore technology, sports, entrepreneurship, and culture. Festivals like Cognizance and Thomso bring students together through innovation, creativity, and performances.

As a Metallurgical and Materials Engineering student, I'm particularly interested in materials research, coding, and solving real-world problems. I see college as an opportunity to experiment, build projects, and explore ideas that connect different fields of engineering.

For me, IIT Roorkee is not simply a place to earn a degree. It is an environment where curiosity can grow into knowledge, ideas can become projects, and challenges can become opportunities to learn.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, and there's so much left to explore. The most exciting part is discovering what I can create along the way.
